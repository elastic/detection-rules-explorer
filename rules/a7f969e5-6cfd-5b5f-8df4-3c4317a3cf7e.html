ESXi Virtual Machine Process List

Last updated 2 days ago on 2026-09-30
Created 2 days ago on 2026-09-30

About

Detects `esxcli vm process list` on an ESXi host. The command lists running virtual machines and the world IDs of their `vmx` processes. Those IDs are what a later kill command uses to stop the VMs and release the locks on their virtual disks.
