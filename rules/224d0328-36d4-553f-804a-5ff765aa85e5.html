ESXi Audit Records Disabled

Last updated 7 days ago on 2026-09-30
Created 7 days ago on 2026-09-30

About

Detects ESXi audit record transmission being turned off, locally or to a remote collector. Audit records are the host's own trail of administrative changes. Disabling them means later changes to accounts, the firewall, and SSH are not recorded there.
