About

Identifies when a GitHub repository is transferred out of an organization. A repository transfer moves the source code, issue history, pull requests, wikis, and settings to another user or organization and removes it from the originating organization's control. Adversaries who have compromised a privileged account or token use outgoing transfers to move intellectual property to an account they control, making this both a collection and an exfiltration technique that can bypass controls focused on cloning or archive downloads.
