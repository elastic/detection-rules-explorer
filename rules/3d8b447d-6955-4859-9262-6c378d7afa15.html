Entra ID FIDO2 Passkey Registered Outside the Self-Service Portal

Last updated a month ago on 2026-10-21
Created a month ago on 2026-10-21

About

Identifies the registration of a FIDO2 or passkey authentication method for a Microsoft Entra ID user that was not initiated by the self-service combined registration portal (Microsoft App Access Panel), My SignIns or My Profile. Registration through Microsoft Graph, a service principal, or another application can indicate scripted provisioning with tooling such as DSInternals.Passkeys or roadtx, which adversaries use after account takeover to register a shadow passkey for phishing-resistant persistence. Administrative provisioning on behalf of users follows the same path and should be baselined.

False Positive Examples
Administrators can legitimately register FIDO2 security keys or passkeys on behalf of users through Microsoft Graph or PowerShell (for example during bulk onboarding or hardware-key provisioning), which also originates outside the self-service portal. Baseline the applications and service principals that legitimately register authentication methods in the environment and add them as exceptions. Investigate registrations initiated by an unexpected application, by a service principal, where the initiator differs from the target user, or that follow an anomalous sign-in or OAuth consent grant.
