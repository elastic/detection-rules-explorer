AWS IAM Password Policy Recon by Rare User

Last updated 16 days ago on 2026-11-11
Created 16 days ago on 2026-11-11

About

Identifies the first time, within a lookback window, an identity retrieves the AWS account password policy via the IAM "GetAccountPasswordPolicy" API. Adversaries with compromised credentials enumerate the password policy to understand credential requirements and plan password spraying, brute force, or new-access-key persistence. This is a New Terms rule detecting a rare occurrence of the "cloud.account.id", "user.name", and "source.as.organization.name" tuple for this action.

False Positive Examples

Query

text code block:
✄𐘗data_stream.dataset: "aws.cloudtrail"
    and event.provider: "iam.amazonaws.com"
    and event.action: "GetAccountPasswordPolicy"
    and event.outcome: "success"
    and source.ip: *
    and cloud.account.id: *
    and user.name: *
    and source.as.organization.name: (
        * and not (
            "Google LLC" or "Amazon.com, Inc." or "Microsoft Corporation" or "GOOGLE-CLOUD-PLATFORM"
            or "MICROSOFT-CORP-MSN-AS-BLOCK" or "AMAZON-AES" or "AMAZON-02"
        )
    )
    and not aws.cloudtrail.user_identity.invoked_by: ("config.amazonaws.com" or "trustedadvisor.amazonaws.com")
