GCP Compute Snapshot Created by Rare User

Last updated 10 days ago on 2026-11-12
Created 10 days ago on 2026-11-12

About

Identifies the first time, within a lookback window, a principal creates a snapshot of a Compute Engine persistent disk ("*.compute.disks.createSnapshot", "*.compute.regionDisks.createSnapshot", or "*.compute.snapshots.insert"). Adversaries create disk snapshots to stage data for exfiltration, copying or sharing the snapshot to an attacker-controlled project without reading the source VM directly. This is a New Terms rule keyed on the project and acting principal, excluding known cloud-provider egress networks.

        *.compute.disks.createSnapshot or *.compute.regionDisks.createSnapshot or *.compute.snapshots.insert

    and not event.outcome: "failure"
    and cloud.project.id: *
    and user.email: *
    and not source.as.organization.name: (
        "Google LLC" or "Amazon.com, Inc." or "Microsoft Corporation" or "GOOGLE-CLOUD-PLATFORM"
