Elastic Security Detection Rules

About

Hunt for infrastructure and identity markers associated with the Java_Ghost threat actor group in AWS CloudTrail logs. Java_Ghost is a financially motivated group targeting AWS environments for phishing (via SES/WorkMail abuse), cryptomining, ransomware, and data exfiltration, with public reporting from Unit 42, Datadog, and Wiz throughout 2025. Documented markers include the creation of EC2 security groups named Java_Ghost, activity from actor-unique IAM identities (Gh0st_808, Gh0st_365, warkopi), and the creation of IAM users matching the group's persistence naming conventions (adminuserdevs, develops, rootdev, ses2). The security group name and the unique identities have no plausible legitimate use; the generic persistence usernames may collide with legitimate identities and require verification of the creating identity and surrounding activity.

✄𐘗from logs-aws.cloudtrail-*
| where @timestamp > now() - 7 day
| where data_stream.dataset == "aws.cloudtrail"
    and event.provider == "ec2.amazonaws.com"
    and event.action == "CreateSecurityGroup"
    and event.outcome == "success"
    and to_lower(group.name) == "java_ghost"
| keep @timestamp, aws.cloudtrail.user_identity.arn, user.name, group.name, source.ip, cloud.region, cloud.account.id
from logs-aws.cloudtrail-*
| where @timestamp > now() - 7 day
| where data_stream.dataset == "aws.cloudtrail"
    and to_lower(user.name) in ("gh0st_808", "gh0st_365", "warkopi")
| stats event_count = count(*), actions = values(event.action), providers = values(event.provider), source_ips = values(source.ip), regions = values(cloud.region)
    by user.name, aws.cloudtrail.user_identity.arn, cloud.account.id
from logs-aws.cloudtrail-*
| where @timestamp > now() - 7 day
| where data_stream.dataset == "aws.cloudtrail"
    and event.provider == "iam.amazonaws.com"
    and event.action == "CreateUser"
    and event.outcome == "success"
| grok aws.cloudtrail.request_parameters "userName=%{DATA:created_user_name}(,|})"
| where to_lower(created_user_name) in ("adminuserdevs", "develops", "rootdev", "ses2", "gh0st_808", "gh0st_365", "warkopi")
| keep @timestamp, aws.cloudtrail.user_identity.arn, user.name, created_user_name, source.ip, cloud.region, cloud.account.id
