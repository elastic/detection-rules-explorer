AWS Bedrock High Tool-Request Call Volume

Last updated 4 days ago on 2026-11-06
Created 18 days ago on 2026-10-23

About

A machine learning job has detected an unusually high number of tool-requesting Amazon Bedrock model responses within 15 minutes for a caller, compared with that caller's own history. High call volume can indicate repeated over-invocation or loop amplification by an AI agent (OWASP ASI02, Tool Misuse and Exploitation) and can lead to resource exhaustion and cost. The volume counts responses that requested tools, not confirmed tool executions.

False Positive Examples
A legitimate spike in agent usage, such as a batch job, a load test, a launch, or a retry storm, can produce a high volume of tool-requesting responses. Confirm with the agent owner. A newly onboarded caller has little history, so early bursts can look unusual until the model has learned its normal range.
