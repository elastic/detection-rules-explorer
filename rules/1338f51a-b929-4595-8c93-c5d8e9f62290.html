Potential Process Self-Deletion

Last updated a day ago on 2026-11-26
Created 17 days ago on 2026-11-10

About

Detects a Linux process deleting its own executable from a relative, temporary, shared-memory, system, web, or user directory. Adversaries may self-delete payloads after execution to remove artifacts and hinder forensic analysis.

Query

text code block:
✄𐘗FROM logs-endpoint.events.file-* METADATA _id, _index, _version
| WHERE KQL("""host.os.type:linux and event.category:file and event.type:deletion and event.action:deletion""")
    AND process.executable IS NOT NULL
    AND file.path IS NOT NULL
    // Require an exact, case-sensitive match between the deleting process executable and the deleted file.
    AND process.executable == file.path
    AND (
      process.executable LIKE "./*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/tmp/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/var/tmp/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/dev/shm/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/run/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/var/run/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/boot/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/sys/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/lost+found/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/proc/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/var/mail/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/var/www/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/home/*/*"
      OR process.executable LIKE "/root/*"
    )
| KEEP
    @timestamp,
    _id,
    _index,
    _version,
    host.id,
    host.name,
    user.id,
    user.name,
    file.path,
    file.name,
    file.hash.sha256,
    process.entity_id,
    process.pid,
    process.name,
    process.executable,
    process.parent.entity_id
